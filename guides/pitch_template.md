# 90-Second Pitch Template

You have 90 seconds on stage. Here is how to fill them.

## Structure (roughly 30 seconds each)

### 1. The problem (30 sec)
- Who are you building for? (the persona)
- What decision can they not make today, and why?
- One sentence on why it matters (dollars, patients, time).

### 2. The demo (30 sec)
- Show the agent answering one question that combines numbers and text.
- Point out where the answer came from (which tool, which table, which document).
- Show the hidden pattern you discovered.

### 3. The impact (30 sec)
- What would change if this agent existed in the real world?
- What would you build next if you had another 90 minutes?
- One sentence on what surprised you.

## Tips
- Rehearse once with your team before going on stage.
- Pick your single best question and answer to show live. Do not try to demo everything.
- If the demo breaks on stage, describe what it did when it worked. The audience will understand.
- Keep it conversational. You are telling a story, not reading a slide.
